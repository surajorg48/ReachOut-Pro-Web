How to Find Decision Maker Emails at Any Company

By ReachOut Pro Team August 12, 2026 6 min read

Whether you're a B2B sales rep, a recruiter, or a freelancer looking for clients, getting in touch with the right person is half the battle. If you're sending emails to generic "info@" or "contact@" addresses, your message is likely getting buried. You need direct access to decision-makers.

1. The Pattern Guessing Method

Most companies use standard email formats for their employees. If you know the person's name and the company's domain, you can often guess their email. Common patterns include:

  • first@company.com
  • first.last@company.com
  • f.last@company.com
  • firstinitiallastname@company.com

While this method is free, testing these addresses manually can be incredibly time-consuming and risks increasing your bounce rate if you guess wrong.

2. Searching Social Media and Professional Networks

LinkedIn is a goldmine for finding decision-makers. You can search for specific job titles (e.g., "VP of Marketing", "CTO") at your target companies. While LinkedIn doesn't always display email addresses publicly, connecting with the prospect can sometimes reveal their contact info.

3. Using Advanced Search Operators

Google can be a powerful email finding tool if you know how to use search operators. Try queries like:

"John Doe" + "company.com" + "email"
"John Doe" + "@company.com"
site:company.com "email"

4. The Most Efficient Solution: Automated Email Extraction

Manual methods are fine for one-off searches, but if you're trying to build a pipeline of hundreds of prospects, you need automation. This is where tools like ReachOut Pro excel.

Automated Extraction

With ReachOut Pro's Email Extraction feature, you can simply input a company domain or use our Lead Discovery tool. The software automatically scrapes the web and cross-references databases to find verified contact information in seconds.

5. The Importance of Email Verification

Finding an email is only step one. Step two is verifying it. Sending emails to invalid addresses damages your sender reputation and can lead to your emails going to the spam folder.

Always use a service that verifies emails (checking MX records, pinging the server) before you hit send. High-quality outreach platforms will build verification directly into the extraction process.

Conclusion

Your time is best spent crafting personalized messages and closing deals, not hunting for email addresses. By leveraging automated extraction and verification tools, you can build high-quality prospect lists quickly and start reaching out with confidence.
